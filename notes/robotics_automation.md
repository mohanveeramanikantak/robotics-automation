# Robotics Automation – Detailed Notes

## 1. Introduction
Robotics Automation refers to the use of robots and intelligent systems to automate tasks traditionally performed by humans.

---

## 2. Components of Robotics Systems
- Sensors
- Controllers
- Actuators
- AI algorithms

---

## 3. Types of Robots

### 3.1 Industrial Robots
Used in manufacturing and assembly lines.

### 3.2 Service Robots
Assist humans in daily activities.

### 3.3 Medical Robots
Used in surgeries and healthcare.

---

## 4. Technologies Used
- Artificial Intelligence
- Machine Learning
- Computer Vision
- IoT

---

## 5. Applications
- Smart factories
- Warehouse management
- Healthcare automation
- Agricultural robotics

---

## 6. Advantages
- Increased productivity
- High accuracy
- Reduced human effort
- Continuous operation

---

## 7. Challenges
- Expensive deployment
- Ethical concerns
- Cybersecurity risks
- Maintenance complexity

---

## 8. Future Scope
- Collaborative robots (Cobots)
- AI-powered automation
- Smart robotic ecosystems

---

## 9. Career Scope
- Robotics Engineer
- AI Developer
- Automation Specialist

---

## 10. Conclusion
Robotics Automation is transforming industries by improving efficiency, safety, and productivity through intelligent systems.
